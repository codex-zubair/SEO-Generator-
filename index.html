Recitals

WHEREAS, Party A is the owner of the license for the mobile application named "SEO Generator" (the "App");

WHEREAS, the App will be developed by Blackly.dev under Shadow.ltd and published on the Google Play Console under Shadow.ltd;

WHEREAS, the Parties wish to enter into a partnership for the development, marketing, and monetization of the App without any initial investment or contribution towards development, marketing, or other relevant costs from the Parties;

WHEREAS, all development, marketing, and other relevant costs for creating, publishing, and promoting the App will be borne and deducted from the gross revenues generated by the App;

WHEREAS, the Parties agree to share the net profits (defined as gross revenues minus all deducted costs including but not limited to development, marketing, publishing, and operational expenses) as follows: 20% to Party A, 40% to Party B, and 40% to Party C;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises and covenants contained herein, the Parties agree as follows:

1. Business Purpose

The purpose of this partnership is to collaborate on the App, including its development, publication, marketing, and monetization. The App will be published under Shadow.ltd's Google Play Console account. Party A provides the license, Party B (through Blackly Official) handles development and operations, and Party C handles marketing efforts, including promotion via the YouTube channel "Game Life-94".

2. Term

The term of this Agreement shall commence and continue indefinitely until terminated by mutual written agreement of the Parties or as provided in Section 9 below.

3. Capital Contributions and Costs

3.1 No initial capital contributions or investments are required from any Party.

3.2 All costs related to the development, marketing, publishing, maintenance, and any other relevant expenses for the App shall be deducted from the gross revenues generated by the App before calculating net profits.

3.3 Party B shall be responsible for tracking and reporting all expenses and revenues transparently to the other Parties.

4. Profit and Loss Distribution

4.1 Net Profits shall be calculated as gross revenues from the App (including but not limited to in-app purchases, advertisements, subscriptions, etc.) minus all deducted costs as described in Section 3.2.

4.2 The Net Profits shall be distributed as follows:

  • 20% to Party A (Niraj, as the app license owner).
  • 40% to Party B (MD Zubair Alam, representing Blackly Official).
  • 40% to Party C (Rupak Debnath).

4.3 Distributions shall be made quarterly, within 30 days after the end of each calendar quarter, or as otherwise agreed by the Parties.

4.4 Losses, if any, shall be borne in the same proportions as profits, but since no investments are made, losses shall be covered from future revenues or as agreed.

5. Roles and Responsibilities

5.1 Party A: Provide the app license and any necessary intellectual property rights for the App.

5.2 Party B: Oversee development, publication under Shadow.ltd, operations, financial tracking, and reporting.

5.3 Party C: Handle marketing and promotion, including using the YouTube channel "Game Life-94" to promote the App.

5.4 All Parties shall act in good faith and cooperate to maximize the success of the App.

6. Ownership and Intellectual Property

6.1 The App license remains owned by Party A.

6.2 Any new intellectual property created during development shall be owned by Blackly Official (Party B), subject to the profit sharing herein.

6.3 The Parties grant each other necessary licenses to perform their roles under this Agreement.

7. Accounting and Records

7.1 Party B shall maintain accurate records of all revenues, expenses, and distributions.

7.2 All Parties shall have the right to inspect the records upon reasonable notice.

7.3 An annual audit may be conducted if requested by any Party, at shared cost.

8. Confidentiality

The Parties agree to keep confidential all proprietary information related to the App and the partnership.

9. Termination

9.1 This Agreement may be terminated by mutual consent or upon material breach by any Party, with 30 days' written notice to cure the breach.

9.2 Upon termination, final distributions shall be made, and ongoing obligations (e.g., confidentiality) shall survive.

10. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of Bangladesh, given the location of Party B and the user.

11. Dispute Resolution

Any disputes shall be resolved through mediation in Dhaka, Bangladesh, or if unsuccessful, through arbitration under the rules of the Bangladesh International Arbitration Centre.

12. Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the Parties and supersedes all prior agreements.

13. Amendments

Any amendments must be in writing and signed by all Parties.

14. Severability

If any provision is invalid, the remainder shall remain in effect.
